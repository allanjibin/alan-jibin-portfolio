Automated Metal Crack Detection.

Engineering a non-contact vision system to ensure industrial chain integrity.

System Concept

The Sketch Explained: This diagram illustrates the core testing rig. The chain moves through an enclosed aluminum/cardboard box designed to stabilize environmental variables. The Arducam is mounted inside to maintain a fixed focal distance, while the interlocked chain passes through the entry and outlet ports. The laptop atop the box processes the stream in real-time, displaying structural data and defect detection alerts directly to the operator.
