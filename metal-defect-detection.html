Thesis / Miniproject 2026

Real-Time Automatic Crack Detection System for Interlocked Metal Chains.

Addressing the critical industrial challenge of reliable, efficient surface defect detection in mechanical systems using automated image processing[cite: 83].

Abstract & Motivation

Industrial metal chains are highly susceptible to surface cracks due to continuous stress, wear, and environmental factors. Traditional manual inspection is inefficient, prone to human fatigue, and unreliable in high-speed production settings[cite: 52, 55]. This system introduces a non-contact, automated approach using computer vision to detect surface defects, ensuring industrial safety and reducing costly downtime[cite: 59, 61].

System Design & Logic

Hardware Block Diagram

The architecture integrates data acquisition (motor, conveyor, camera, lighting) with a processing unit (Python/OpenCV/YOLOv8) to drive output interfaces, including visual defect overlays, audible alerts, and conveyor stop signals[cite: 481, 482].

Firmware Flowchart

The system initializes the YOLOv8 model and camera, then enters a continuous scan loop. AI inference occurs at a confidence threshold of 0.15; if cracks are detected, the system updates the safety status, increments the defect counter, and ultimately logs all data to a CSV file for maintenance analysis[cite: 549, 588].

Dataset Management

The detection model was trained using 56 high-resolution images, meticulously annotated in Roboflow to classify surface defects across interlocked chain links[cite: 502].

Performance Evaluation

Live Detection

The system provides real-time visual feedback, marking "DEFECTIVE" status and bounding boxes around cracks with high confidence[cite: 637].

Data Logging

All results are automatically logged with date, time, and defect counts, facilitating long-term equipment health monitoring[cite: 700, 706].
